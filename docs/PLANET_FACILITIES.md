# Military planets and facilities

**Single Player → PLANETARY OVERWATCH AND FACILITIES** opens a Taiidan Industrial World with Hardened Defences, Ion Batteries and Missile Defence. Battery Coordinator is attached to the Ion Batteries. The opposing frigates are synthetic training profiles; the listed planet/facility/leader values come from supplied sources.

| Profile | Implemented values |
| --- | --- |
| Industrial World | One model, 5+ success, armor 5, 4 stars, stationary; 6 defensive dice only |
| Hardened Defences | 2 stars, armor +2, destroyed before other facilities |
| Ion Batteries | 3 stars, Heavy Ion Cannon 6 dice/range 3, Penetrating 1, Power Penetrating 2, cannot target fighters |
| Missile Defence | 3 stars, Ballistic Missiles 6 dice/range 3, Power 8 dice, projectile defensive fire applies |
| Taiidan Battery Coordinator | 3 stars, Hand 3, Play 2, +1 attack die only for its own facility; Planetary Overwatch |

## Orders

Select a battery in the weapon picker **before** starting an Attack order. Each weapon facility spends one card/Play and activates once per round independently of the other battery. Each ordinary activation offers free Damage Control, including repair of that selected battery's Crippled-Weapons. Other batteries remain ready until activated. Base planetary defensive batteries cannot make ordinary attacks.

Planetary Overwatch responds to enemy Move, Lightning Move or Attack with one Power Attack from the Hero's bound live, functioning facility, only against that acting enemy. It spends the once-per-scenario Heroic card, with no Play, ordinary activation or activation repairs. Used batteries may react. Counter and nested enemy reactions retain normal timing; losing the bound facility/leader cancels the shot and restores the original order's continuation.

## Damage

The military planet is one target over its physical map token. Calculate armor once for the incoming attack. Each remaining success removes one facility chosen by the attacker, Hardened first. Its leader and scoped damage disappear with it; printed facility/leader stars go to the opponent. If the final success removes the last facility, the military body remains; a further success removes that body and awards its base stars. The physical planet always remains, blocks movement and blocks lines behind it. Direct attacks may end their line on the military planet itself.

Crippled-Weapons selects a surviving weapon facility. Engines has no effect. Ordinary boarding/capture of planets is unavailable. A non-fighter ram retains the planet's armor at impact for its return collision, even if the original hit destroys Hardened Defences. Damage choices, repairs and nested reactions are public saved/networked decisions owned by the appropriate player.

## Preparation and limits

The fleet editor buys the current Industrial World bundle for 12 stars (4 + 2 + 3 + 3); adding the Coordinator totals 15. Assign the leader to an explicit facility with the facility picker. Prepared deployment creates the physical planet token. Facility purchase/reconfiguration is not yet a separate editor operation.

This completes functional support for all fourteen **received Heroic command types**. It is the first planetary slice, not complete Campaign Expansion support. Remaining work includes other planet variants/facilities (Starport, Orbital Defence Control, Minefield), orbital factory/launch behavior, full planetary capture/bombardment exceptions, campaign ownership/purchases and remaining exact attachment profiles. Existing terrain minefields and ship factories remain separate mechanics.

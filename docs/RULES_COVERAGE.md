## Source gap

The three `HOMEWORLD-*` card PDFs in the supplied G: directory are blank design templates. Component catalogues list names and counts but do not provide the actual card effects/stat blocks. The errata links the official downloads page, which is protected by an access code. The user will provide photographs later and explicitly requested continuing with the written rules now. Exact missing card/leader/unit/hero data must not be guessed from a title or an AI behavior summary. This does not block independent rulebook mechanics.

Card artwork is a separate optional presentation pack, selected in Options. It is excluded from gameplay fingerprints and can be removed without invalidating saves. The supplied blank frames were rendered into the ignored local cache; the repository contains only the importer and a color-only example pack.

## Delivery rule

Every completed row needs authoritative command validation, legal-action previews where applicable, immutable save/resume coverage, network/privacy checks for new hidden data and a usable UI action. Engine-only support is recorded separately from a playable feature. Cosmetic cube rendering remains in scope until the rules are complete.

## Recorded interpretations and boundaries

- Numeric Focus Fire is represented as canonical two-point tokens plus a possible one-point token. Kushan repair removes an entire token (up to two points), following the FAQ.
- A successful scenario-authorized boarding capture retains the last model, transfers control and preserves its activation. One Crew Damage is paid by the boarding unit. Scenario capture medals are now supported by objective definitions.
- Defensive fire and collision do not consume a normal attack window or replace the unit's last ordered action. The boarding lock checks the defender's last ordered action, opponent and round.
- Barrage uses its own success threshold, ignores Evasive and adds no model/leader/support bonus dice. Ordinary reductions still apply; defensive fire ignores terrain. Planet/facility pools remain pending.
- Missing one Stay in Formation action line is permitted. Without a surviving assigned first unit, the second has no adjacency anchor for Lightning Move, but can skip it and attack.
- Entering CAP at the end of a move skips any remaining actions assigned to that fighter in the same order. CAP and its host must be ready to exit before a new order. Interception uses a card and no Play; dogfight pools are both rolled before either side chooses rerolls, and casualties apply simultaneously. The more specific p.25 sequence permits hull defensive fire after CAP.
- CAP interception uses ordinary ranged attacks for Valor/Ferocious. `capSuccess` can supply the Defence Fighter's post-activation threshold from the errata. Source clarification is still needed for capture/recovery of a host with an enemy patrol attached; these two control-transfer operations are currently unavailable while that patrol remains. Destroying the host returns the patrol normally.
- Current close/defense tests use synthetic statistics to force timing cases. They do not introduce guessed published cards into the playable roster. Unknown card effects remain waiting for the user's photos.

- Terrain profiles use the Asteroid Field/Minefield cards reproduced in v020 (pp. 22 and 36): 3 dice for asteroids or 1 per mine, success 5+, Penetrating 1. US Digital p.28 supplies the current sequence and Focus Fire reduction. A mine reduction earned by hull defensive fire is applied after that mine attack resolves, following the older printed card; that pending attack retains the current token's pool. Inflicting casualties and/or Focus Fire is one further reduction, allowing two reductions in one activation. CAP exchanges resolve before the separate parent attack (p.25).
- Activation damage control completes before queued terrain attacks; surviving actors then resume their paid order. Entry into an allied occupied hex retains a separate transit coordinate so saves never overlap physical units. Turning in place triggers activation only. US Digital p.13 credits destroyed enemy units through any means: neutral terrain also awards the opposing fleet its destroyed-star value. Crew/leader transfer is deferred until activation repair and terrain finish.

- Harvest is one of the four basic orders (US Digital p.14), not an invented special card. Its optional free Move and subsequent single Harvest form one saved order, spending one card/Play and activating only the collector. Any unit on the asteroid hex does not prevent harvesting (p.18). Movement reactions and entry attacks finish before the Harvest choice; a destroyed mover reveals/collects nothing. Skipping the free move does not trigger Survey.
- Survey occurs after movement finishes and reveals every adjacent deposit only to that fleet. Maps specify a public multiset of token values, shuffled privately at setup. Unknown values and the opponent's knowledge bits are redacted from client snapshots, including campaign battles. Depleted tokens remain dangerous asteroid terrain and cannot be harvested again, including zero-star tokens. Cargo is public, lost with its unit, and independent of destroyed-star score; cumulative harvested totals are retained for future objectives.
- Star transfer spends a card/Play and activates the chosen sender or recipient; the adjacent allied partner may already be activated. Repair and activation terrain resolve before cargo changes. The economy training collectors deliberately combine configurable Harvest/Survey for testing and are not published unit cards. Factory spending and reserve pools are now implemented; harvesting medal objectives are now implemented; published scenario packs remain pending.

- Reinforce is a paid basic order with saved launch, deployment or factory placement. US Digital p.19 and Errata 1.7 FAQ p.6 leave the parent unactivated when disembarking; the implementation also allows an already activated parent to launch and applies the same parent rule to factory output. Launch Tubes is shared across the round, not reset by another Reinforce. A factory build creates one whole unit per basic order (the singular build sequence on p.19), at its defined star cost; launch orders can release multiple embarked units up to capacity.
- A frigate/capital reserve arrives in its configured setup zone; attached strike craft stay embarked. Factory output may go directly into a free bay, following the FAQ's blocked-exit clarification. Crippled Bays prevents disembarking, while boarding into or manufacturing inside a free bay remains possible. Arriving units count as activated, clear earlier buffs and can take chosen activation Damage Control before entry terrain resolves.
- Embarking reuses movement paths and Interrupt Attack windows, with a final one-move bay entry. Capacity, strike class, side and remaining movement are validated; entering a bay cancels that actor's remaining actions. No nested embarked carriers are supported. Preset scenarios ignore tab colors as p.19 permits; free-form colored tabs/force selection remain pending.
- Destroying a carrier removes its embarked units and awards their printed ship/leader stars, alongside the parent. Neutral terrain also credits the opposing fleet for the parent and embarked losses (US Digital p.13). Leaders off board contribute no Hand/Play until the round after entering play. Reserves prevent prototype elimination while deployable forces remain. Control transfer of a parent with embarked enemy units is unavailable pending source clarification, as with the existing hostile-CAP transfer boundary.

- Scenario victory follows US Digital p.13 and the FAQ p.6: each objective can be scored once; three medals end the battle immediately. At round exhaustion compare medals, then destroyed-unit stars, then draw. Harvested resources/cargo/reserve funds do not break ties. Objective maps use this system instead of prototype elimination. Legacy maps keep their previous victory rules.
- Capture objectives require a successful boarding capture, not initial ownership or a crew recovery. A `reversible` capture objective follows the last successful capturer (p.26): recapture removes/restores its existing medals without farming new copies. A destroyed captured target does not transfer those medals merely by disappearing. The default permanent objective retains its award.
- Thresholds and target IDs are scenario data. Destroyed-star objectives may filter unit classes; destroyed children and leaders are recorded with their unit cost. Specific-target and threshold medals may be earned on the same kill. Medal victory stops subsequent order actions, incoming attacks after defensive fire, and return collision after a winning ram. Simultaneous CAP casualties both apply before checking medals; simultaneous thresholds use medals/stars/draw to avoid processing-order bias.
- Campaign mission results store both sides' actually earned medals, including defeat/draw, along with a result hash and round. The same finished battle cannot be counted twice, even for a later mission using the same map. Explicit `onDraw` falls back to the existing defeat branch when omitted. This is base mission accounting, not the Campaign Expansion's purchases or major/minor-objective final victory rules.
